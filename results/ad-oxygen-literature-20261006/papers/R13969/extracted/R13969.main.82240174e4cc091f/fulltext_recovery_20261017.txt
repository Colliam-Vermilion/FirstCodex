Thesis Advisor: Prof. Sumaeth Chavadej 74 pp.
Keywords: 
Two stage hydrogen and methane production/ Cassava wastewater/
Microaeration/ Upflow anaerobic sludge blanket reactor (UASB)/ 
Mesophilic operation
In this work, a two stage upflow anaerobic sludge blanket (UASB) system 
was used to produce separately hydrogen and methane from cassava wastewater 
under mesophilic temperature (37 °C) with a recycle ratio of 1:1 (final effluent flow 
rate: feed flow rate) and a constant pH of 5.5 in the hydrogen UASB unit. The liquid 
working volumes of the hydrogen and methane UASB units were 4 and 24 L, 
respectively. For the first part without microaeration, For the first part without 
microaeration, the optimum COD loading rate was found to be 6 kg-m'3,d~' based on 
the methane UASB volume to provide the highest process performance, the produced 
gas contained 34 %H2, 65 %C02, 1 %CH4, the hydrogen yield of 45 mF-kgf'COD 
removed and the COD removal of 37% for the hydrogen UASB volume. For the 
methane UASB volume was compose of 63 %CH4, 36.6 %CO2, 0.4 %H2, the 
methane yield of 652.6 mF-kg''COD removed and the COD removal of 52.0%. At 
this optimum COD loading rate, The system was operated at different oxygen supply 
rate (ranging from 5.2 to 7.9 mL 02-L'1wastewater'd'1), the optimum oxygen supply rate 
of 6.1 mF 02-L’1 wastewater'd'1 was found to provide the highest improvement of 
process performance in terms of the highest methane content of 67%, the highest 
methane yield of 682.3 mF-kg'1COD removed and the highest COD removal of 
57.7%. Moreover, the addition of oxygen was found to eliminate completely H2ร in 
the produced gas from the methane UASB unit.
